Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

【请填写姓名或团队名称 / Your name or team】 (“we”) takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what Classic Sports Car Club collects, how it is used, and what rights you have.

1. Information we collect

• Account information: your email and password (passwords are encrypted by the authentication service; we cannot see them in plain text). • Content you post: photos, car model, year, description and comments. • Nickname: the public name you choose. The system generates a random nickname when you sign up, and you can change it at any time. • Report records: reports you submit and the reasons. • Technical information: our service provider may process necessary network logs, such as IP addresses, to keep the service running and secure. When you upload a photo, we only receive the one you choose.

2. What we don't do

As of the date above, the App shows no ads, uses no third-party advertising or behavioral analytics tools, does not sell personal information, and does not collect your precise location or contacts.

3. How we use information

To provide sign-up and login, display your content, handle reports and keep the community orderly, keep the service secure, and reply to your questions.

4. What is public

Photos, car details, comments and nicknames you post are visible to everyone, including visitors who are not logged in. Do not include personal information in public content that you don't want others to see. Your email and password are never shown publicly.

5. Third-party services

We use Supabase for authentication, database and file storage. Your data is stored on its servers and handled under its own privacy policy. We do not share your personal information with other third parties except as needed to provide the service or required by law.

6. Storage and retention

Data is stored on servers in Japan (Tokyo). We keep it for as long as needed to provide the service. When you delete content or your account, the related data is deleted, although data we are legally required to keep, and backups, may take a reasonable time to be cleared.

7. Your rights and account deletion

You can delete content you posted at any time in the App. On the Me page, tap Delete account to permanently delete your account and all the cars, photos, comments and nickname you posted; this cannot be undone. If you cannot do this in the App, or want to access or correct your personal information, email 【请填写联系邮箱 / Your contact email】.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who become aware of this should contact us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will notify you in the App of significant changes. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions? Contact 【请填写联系邮箱 / Your contact email】.
